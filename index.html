Great design communicates. Greater design engages.

I’m a graphic designer interested in the meeting point of education, entertainment, and exploration.

I’m especially interested in imagery as a kind of universal language. Images can carry both information and feeling, sometimes without relying on spoken language at all.

My work moves between print, digital design, code, 3D, and virtual reality. I like figuring out how layout, color, hierarchy, movement, and interaction can change the way someone understands something.

I’m a left-and-right-brain designer. I like the creative side of design, but I also like figuring out how things actually work.
